This page helps you do one thing: **choose one course that truly fits you right now.** You do not need to collect certificates first or sign up for five courses at once. Choose one, build something you can show, and then take the next step.

To follow this project and build step by step, return to the [main learning path](../README.en.md); for unfamiliar terms, see the [glossary](glossary.en.md).

## 🧩 First, separate five terms that are easy to mix up

<div align="center" markdown="1">

| Core term | The five-year-old version | What it actually means |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Course** | A teacher has laid out a learning path. | A set of videos, articles, exercises, or projects arranged in sequence. |
| **Certificate of Completion** | Proof that you made it through the course. | Proof that you completed the assigned content; it is not a degree and does not by itself prove that you can build a production system. |
| **Skill Badge** | A little sticker showing that you completed a task. | A digital badge issued by a platform for a short module or a specific skill. |
| **Professional Certificate** | Several courses packaged as one bigger learning bundle. | A certificate for a series of courses designed by a company or school; it is usually still not a degree or a license. |
| **Certification Exam** | You do more than take the course—you also sit another exam. | An exam through which a vendor or testing organization verifies knowledge of a specific product; it may require payment, identity verification, or periodic renewal. |

</div>

**The most important rule: a certificate proves that you completed a path; your work shows other people what you can do.**

## 🧪 Leave the same piece of work evidence after every course

Do not just download a PDF. Copy this small card and record checkable results for every course:

```text
The problem I solved:
Tools my Agent can use:
How I know it worked:
How it stops safely when it fails:
Runnable code or Demo link:
```

The smallest project can be an Agent that looks up information, calls one tool, and records an Eval result. When you finish, return to this project and compare it with [Stage 3 Tool Use](../stages/03-tool-use-and-hello-agent.en.md), [Stage 4 Workflow Graph](../stages/04-agent-frameworks.en.md), and [Stage 7 Production Engineering](../stages/07-multi-agent-production.en.md).

<details markdown="1">
<summary>📜 Expand: What does a certificate actually prove?</summary>

1. **A Certificate of Completion only proves that you completed the assigned steps.** It is not a degree and does not guarantee that you can independently ship an Agent.
2. **A certification exam is different from a Certificate of Completion.** It may require proctoring, identity verification, and a separate fee; do not write a free course badge as a professional license.
3. **Free does not mean poor, and paid does not guarantee a good fit.** First check whether the course includes exercises, Eval, projects, and current documentation.
4. **Put your work on your resume too.** Honestly write what you completed, what you built, and how you tested it; do not just paste a row of badges.
5. **Courses change.** Before signing up, reconfirm the cost, certificate requirements, language, and any API / cloud accounts you need.

</details>

<summary>🔎 Expand: How is this list maintained?</summary>

- Repository stars are used only to discover community attention and are not written into the main text; maintenance status comes from whether the repository is archived, its recent updates, and its current documentation.
