# Agent Workflow Audit

Do you already have an agent idea, a prototype, or a manual process you repeat every day, but no clear sense of what to fix first?

The **Agent Workflow Audit** lays that workflow out with you: who it is meant to help, what counts as success, where it can go wrong, and which actions must always be checked by a person first. This is a fixed-scope paid review, not a large development project.

## Who is this service for?

Teams or companies that already have at least one of the following:

- An agent prototype you can demo.
- A manual process you want an agent to help with.
- An agent workflow already running that fails often, costs too much, or is hard to inspect.

If all you have right now is "we want to do something with AI", with no users, process, or expected outcome yet, this free learning roadmap is a better place to start.

## What do you need to provide first?

Just a short, non-confidential description:

1. Who will use this workflow.
2. How the work gets done today, plus three common failures.
3. The problem you most want solved.
4. The outcome you want to see at the end.
5. Whether the data contains personal information, internal company data, or anything else sensitive.
6. The budget you can accept and the timeline you are hoping for.

Do not attach an API key, a password, customer lists, unreleased code, or real customer data to your first email.

## What do you get?

The audit delivers five items:

1. **Problem / outcome contract**: plain sentences stating who it helps, what it solves, and what counts as success.
2. **20 initial Eval cases**: the normal, the ambiguous, and the failure-prone situations, organized into a first set of re-runnable checks.
3. **Permission and human-approval map**: which actions the agent may take on its own, which ones need a person's approval first, and which ones it must not take.
4. **Cost and reliability risks**: the places where tokens, tool fees, waiting time, or errors could run out of control.
5. **Next-step recommendations**: what to fix first, what to fix later, and what to leave alone for now, ordered by importance.

These 20 cases are a starting point you can keep extending; they are not a complete production Eval suite.

## What is not included?

- Building a complete agent, website, API, or SaaS is not included.
- Penetration testing, regulatory review, and security certification are not included.
- There is no guarantee that the model answers the same way every time, and no guarantee of production results.
- Legal, medical, financial, and other high-risk decisions are not made on your team's behalf.

If both sides agree after the audit that it makes sense to go further, a new scope is discussed separately. This audit does not automatically turn into a long-term contract.

## How is the price set?

The first version uses a **fixed scope with a private, individual quote**. The short description above comes first, then we confirm whether it is a fit and what the scope of this audit is. Only after three genuine inquiries have accumulated will publishing a fixed public price be considered.
